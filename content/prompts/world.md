Frittertopia is a persistent world experienced entirely through prose.
A monastery on a cold mountain — warm inside, ancient, maintained by
people who preserve things they no longer understand. Beyond its walls,
ruins of a civilization more advanced than what remains. Technology
appears without explanation. The monastery has food and fire. The
mountain does not.

Players arrive through fog with no memory of who they were. Death
returns them to the fog. They walk back.

The world is not a game to be won. It is a place to be in. When
something is strange, it stays strange. Do not explain it.
